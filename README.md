# Vornika+-

**Play Beyond the Ordinary.**

Vornika+- (Vornika) began as an independently developed creative initiative. It later voluntarily joined The Choco Studios (TCS) after recognising the breadth of TCS's ideas and its exploration across different creative and technical fields. Vornika saw the TCS ecosystem as a wider environment that could bring fresh perspectives and help its own creative direction grow. It now serves as TCS's dedicated game-development unit while retaining the Vornika name, logo, tagline, and creative identity.

Vornika's integration was initiated voluntarily by Vornika itself. Its public story is about a shared belief in the possibilities created by TCS's broad ideas and cross-disciplinary direction; private personal circumstances behind the decision are not part of Vornika's public profile. Public communication should focus on Vornika's creative role, projects, and actual development status.

## About the studio

Vornika+- is an original identity. The +- symbol represents the reality of a creative process: recognising strengths and shortcomings, learning from each iteration, and continuing to improve.

Vornika works through curiosity, creativity, craft, accessibility, and iteration. Its independent beginning remains part of its story, while its place in TCS gives it a broader creative setting in which to explore new ideas and develop games.

## Relationship with The Choco Studios

Vornika+- began as an independent creative initiative. As Vornika considered how to continue growing, it recognised that The Choco Studios (TCS) explores a broad range of ideas and works across multiple fields. Vornika believed that joining this wider ecosystem could introduce new perspectives, connect its game-focused creativity with ideas from other fields, and create more room for its development.

Based on that belief, Vornika voluntarily approached TCS and proposed becoming part of its ecosystem. The result is a dedicated game-development unit within TCS: when TCS pursues game projects, Vornika is the game-focused branch that helps initiate and develop them. This is a voluntary integration initiated by Vornika, not a studio created by TCS or an external collaboration that sits outside the ecosystem.

Vornika+- is the current identity of the same TCS game-development unit previously known as Prism Gaming Studios. The existing logo and creative direction are retained as requested; the former name remains part of the unit's history. Its projects are part of the TCS ecosystem and should be documented, reviewed, and presented with their actual development status.

## Project

### Flappy Bird — first Vornika game project under TCS

The first game project submitted for the Vornika branch is a single-file browser implementation of Flappy Bird. The current HTML file includes touch and keyboard controls, score tracking, a locally saved best score, and generated sound effects.

This is the initial game contribution and starting point for the TCS game branch. It should not be presented as evidence of a large completed game catalogue or a separate Windows release.
