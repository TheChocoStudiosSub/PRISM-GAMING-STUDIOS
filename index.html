01 — ABOUT PRISM

Turning curiosity into play.

Prism explores interactive ideas through creative development and continuous learning. Each project is an opportunity to test a new perspective, understand what makes an experience engaging, and create something worth returning to. We remain open to different genres, styles, mechanics, and technologies.

02 — OUR PLACE IN THE TCS ECOSYSTEM

Connected by a shared vision.

Prism Gaming Studios chose to become part of The Choco Studios (TCS) ecosystem because it is genuinely interested in TCS's vision and mission: building an open, collaborative ecosystem where ideas, knowledge, and creative work can grow across different fields. TCS's commitment to open-source development and shared progress aligns with Prism's belief that creativity becomes stronger when people can learn from one another, exchange perspectives, and build together.

This connection gives Prism room to contribute to a wider creative environment while continuing to operate with its own name, identity, creative direction, and approach to game development. Being part of the ecosystem is a shared commitment, not a loss of independence.
